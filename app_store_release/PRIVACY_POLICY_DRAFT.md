## Summary
Pixel Polo is a single-player water polo game. It has no accounts, and we don't ask for your name,
email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Your game progress stays on your device.
Ads in the game are shown by Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one.

## Information stored on your device
Your dynasties, settings, Polo Points, unlocks and a record of your purchases (product and
transaction ids) are saved only on your device. We don't receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Purchases
In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the
game which product you bought, so it can deliver it and restore one-time unlocks.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
If you choose to watch an ad for a Lucky Spin, the ad is provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect
device information (such as an app-specific device identifier, IP address and approximate location
derived from it), ad interactions, and diagnostic data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
We don't request permission to track you, so the game doesn't give AdMob your device's advertising
identifier (IDFA). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the
game asks for your consent choices before any ad is requested. You can change them [in the consent
form / by reinstalling]. Google's practices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
